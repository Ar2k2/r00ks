How the house designer works

Start from a preset like the cottage, barn or stone hall, then change anything. The roof works like the roof generator, gable or hip with an overhang past the walls and separate blocks for the roof and its trim, but you can give it any shape. Set the wall height, a base row and corner pillars, logs or stone. Drag to turn the house, scroll or pinch to zoom, and use the layer slider to watch the roof go up layer by layer. When you change something, only the blocks that are new appear; everything else stays put.

How do I shape the roof?

Pick a shape to start from: 45°, shallow or steep, a gambrel (steep, then shallow, like a barn), a mansard (steep sides and a flat top), a bell (flared eaves and a steep crown) or flat. Then draw on the cross-section under it: click or drag in a row to set where that layer's edge sits, counting from the eaves up. The roof follows as you draw. A layer that steps in one block from the one below gets stairs, two or more gets slabs, and one that rises straight up gets full blocks. Grab the top of the roof and drag it up or down to change its height. Height sets how many layers tall the roof is: the shape stretches to fit, getting steeper as it gets taller and shallower as it gets lower.

Where do the windows and door go?

Windows go every third block along each wall, starting two blocks in from a corner, one block above the floor (or above the base row). Walls five or more blocks high get windows two blocks tall. The door sits in the middle of the front wall. Turn either off with the switches under the walls.

How do I see real Minecraft textures?

Load your own Minecraft jar or a resource pack. The textures belong to Mojang, so this site doesn't include them; your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Without one, each block shows its average color. Each version you have played has a jar in the launcher's versions folder, for example versions/26.3/26.3.jar.

Can I share a design?

Copy link saves the whole design in a link you can send to a friend. Save image downloads a picture of it, and Spin turns it slowly like a turntable. The counts under the controls list every block for the roof and the walls, so you know what to gather.
