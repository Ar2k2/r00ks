How the 3D designer works

Pick what to build at the top: a house, a dome or an arch. Start from a preset, then shape it by dragging the model itself. Hover over the build and the part a drag would change lights up. Drag it sideways to move an edge in or out, or up and down to change a height. Drag the empty space around the build to turn it, and scroll or pinch to zoom. The sliders do the same things when you want an exact number. When something changes, only the new blocks pop in; everything else stays put.

How do I shape a roof?

The roof works like the roof generator: gable or hip, with an overhang past the walls and separate blocks for the roof and its trim. Pick a shape to start from: 45°, shallow or steep, a gambrel (steep, then shallow, like a barn), a mansard (steep sides and a flat top), a bell (flared eaves and a steep crown) or flat. Then grab a layer of the roof and drag it in or out to set where that layer's edge sits. The layers above follow, so pulling an edge in lowers the roof. A layer that steps in one block from the one below gets stairs, two or more gets slabs, and one that rises straight up gets full blocks. Drag the roof up or down to change its height: the shape stretches to fit. Drag a wall outward to make the house wider or longer, or up to make it taller.

How do domes work?

A dome sits on a round wall, which can be zero blocks tall for a dome on the ground. Round is half a sphere, matching the circle and sphere generator. Shallow is lower, pointed rises to a tip, and onion swells out past its base before drawing in to a point. Grab any layer of the dome and drag it in or out: the curve bends smoothly around it, so you can pinch a waist or flare the bottom. Drag the dome up or down for its height, and the wall outward to make it wider.

How do arches work?

An arch has straight legs and a curved top: round, pointed, shallow (a flat curve, like a bridge) or flat (a plain doorway). Drag the inside of the curve to reshape it, the top up or down for the rise, the legs for their height, the outside to make it thicker and the front to make it deeper.

Where do the windows and door go?

Windows go every third block along each wall, starting two blocks in from a corner, one block above the floor (or above the base row). Walls five or more blocks high get windows two blocks tall. The door sits in the middle of the front wall. Turn either off with the switches under the walls.

How do I see real Minecraft textures?

Load your own Minecraft jar or a resource pack. The textures belong to Mojang, so this site doesn't include them; your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Without one, each block shows its average color. Each version you have played has a jar in the launcher's versions folder, for example versions/26.3/26.3.jar.

Can I share a design?

Copy link saves the whole design in a link you can send to a friend. Save image downloads a picture of it, and Spin turns it slowly like a turntable. The counts under the controls list every block, so you know what to gather.
