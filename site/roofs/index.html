How the roof and arch generator works

Both open in 3D, which you can drag to turn; switch to Layers or Front for a flat plan to build from. A roof's flat plan is seen from above, one layer at a time, starting on top of your walls; in 3D the layer you are on is highlighted. Each layer steps in from the edge by the slope you pick: 45° steps in one block per layer, shallow steps in two, and steep rises two layers before stepping in. A gable roof slopes on the two long sides and leaves flat ends for you to fill with wall; a hip roof slopes on all four sides. Width is the side the roof slopes across, and length runs along the ridge.

An arch is shown from the front, from where it starts to curve up to its top. Round arches follow half an ellipse, a perfect half circle when the height is half the width. Pointed arches are two curves that meet in a point, like a church window; they need a height of at least half the width. The faint blocks are the opening you walk through.

How do I build a diagonal arch?

Turn on Diagonal to see the arch running at 45° across the ground, in 3D; drag it to look around. Build it from the front view one column at a time, but move each column one block over and one block back from the last, so the arch follows the faint path on the ground. Diagonal blocks only touch at their edges, so fill in beside them if you want the arch to look solid from every side.

Where do stairs and slabs go?

Stairs are drawn with their tall back half solid and their low step faint, so each one faces out over its faint side, down the slope. A stair on two sides at once is a corner stair on a hip roof; place two stairs next to each other and Minecraft shapes the corner for you. Shallow roofs get a bottom slab on each edge with a full block behind it, and steep roofs alternate a full block and a stair as they climb. The counts list blocks, stairs and slabs separately, so you know what to craft.

What does hollow mean for a roof?

Hollow keeps only the blocks you see from outside, so the roof is a shell with an attic underneath. Turn it off for a solid roof, which takes many more blocks.
