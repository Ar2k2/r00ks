How the roof and arch generator works

A roof is shown from above, one layer at a time, starting on top of your walls. Each layer steps in from the edge by the slope you pick: 45° steps in one block per layer, shallow steps in two, and steep rises two layers before stepping in. A gable roof slopes on the two long sides and leaves flat ends for you to fill with wall; a hip roof slopes on all four sides. Width is the side the roof slopes across, and length runs along the ridge.

An arch is shown from the front, from where it starts to curve up to its top. Round arches follow half an ellipse, a perfect half circle when the height is half the width. Pointed arches are two curves that meet in a point, like a church window; they need a height of at least half the width. The faint blocks are the opening you walk through.

Where do stairs and slabs go?

The blocks on the outer edge of each roof layer are where the slope shows, so that is where stairs go on a 45° roof and slabs on a shallow one. Corners of a hip roof take corner stairs, which Minecraft shapes for you when you place two stairs side by side. The count shows full blocks, so swap in stairs or slabs one for one.

What does hollow mean for a roof?

Hollow keeps only the blocks you see from outside, so the roof is a shell with an attic underneath. Turn it off for a solid roof, which takes many more blocks.
