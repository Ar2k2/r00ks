About the world tools

Nether portal coordinate calculator

The Nether is scaled 8 to 1 against the Overworld on X and Z. Convert coordinates both ways, rounded the way the game does it, to link two portals without them connecting to the wrong one.

Stronghold finder

Throw an Eye of Ender from two different spots, enter your coordinates and facing angle from F3, and the two lines cross at the stronghold. You also get the distance and the Nether coordinates for a shorter trip.

Slime chunk finder

Enter your world seed to see a map of the slime chunks around you and the nearest one, using the same calculation the game runs. Text seeds work too.
