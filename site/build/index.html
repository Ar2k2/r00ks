About the building tools

Circle and sphere generator

Block-by-block circles, spheres and domes from 1 to 128 blocks across, with no flat spots or odd bumps. Spheres and domes are shown one layer at a time, on a grid that is easy to count.

Roof and arch generator

Gable and hip roofs at different slopes, layer by layer, plus round and pointed arches. Turn them in 3D or follow a flat plan.

House designer

Design a whole house in 3D: roof shape, walls, corner pillars, trim, windows and a door. Load your own game jar to see it with real textures, read only in your browser.

Beacon pyramid calculator

How many blocks each beacon pyramid level needs, from one level to the full four, with the range and the powers each level unlocks.
