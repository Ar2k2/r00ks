Color & art

Tools built on block colors measured from the game: gradients, palettes, pictures made of blocks, banners and fireworks.

Gradient: Smooth block gradients between any two colors.Palette: Build block palettes that sit well together.Image mosaic: Turn a picture into a wall or floor of blocks.Map art: Turn an image into a map art block layout.Banner designer: Banners layer by layer, with loom steps and commands.Firework designer: Firework stars and rockets, with crafting steps.

About the color tools

Block gradient generator

Pick two colors and get the smoothest row of Minecraft Java blocks between them. Every full, solid block face has a measured average color, and the whole row is scored at once so each step looks even, with filters for survival-only blocks, wool, gravity blocks and light sources. The gradient guide explains which blocks blend well and which to avoid.

Block palette generator

Start from one block or color and get a set of blocks that sit well together, chosen with color harmony in OKLab, for walls, roofs and floors.

Image to blocks converter

Turn a picture into a pixel art wall or floor of blocks, 16 to 256 blocks wide, with a count of every block you need in stacks of 64.

Map art generator

Turn any image into map art, flat or staircased for more shades, with a block list and a one-command build. Your image never leaves your browser.

Banner designer

Design a banner layer by layer with all 42 patterns and 16 dye colors, then follow the loom steps or copy a give command.

Firework designer

Design firework stars and rockets: shapes, colors, fades, trail and twinkle. See each burst, get every crafting step in order and the materials to gather.
